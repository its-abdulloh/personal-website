Books

I've never liked reading growing up. i played video games, sports, watched tv. The idea of looking at a page with a bunch of letters was never interesting to me as a kid. As i started growing i started to read more tho not books but articles, then i tried a children's book just for fun called "The diary of a wimpy kid". It was so fun to read through, it kinda like tv show but on a page with letters, what's cooler than that? Then i picked up "Harry Potter", it was way more complex in story yet twice as engaging, you don't know what to expect the world just builds itself right before your eyes. Then after that i read 10 or a dozen of books, i don't claim to be a heavy reader but i read from time to time. The reason i read is that books enable you to live a different life in yout head.

Favorites

  • 1984 by George Orwell
  • Fahrenheit 451 by Rad Bradbury
